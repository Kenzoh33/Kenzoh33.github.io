About

I like building things, and I like automating things even more. Give me a task I have to repeat, and I'm already looking for a way to avoid doing it a third time. People who know me tend to call me good company, someone who takes the work seriously without taking himself too seriously.

Table tennis is where that competitive streak really shows. I'm better than I let on, and if you challenge me, I won't go easy on you. Soccer these days is more of a spectator habit than an active one, though I'll never turn down a pickup game. Outside of that, I'm almost always listening to hip-hop, and there's usually a trip somewhere new in the works.
